    The proposal

    Netflix already runs a games catalog inside its subscription. A short, controlled GTA VI trial would be the biggest game demo ever offered there, and Rockstar keeps full control of when and how it happens.

    00:00 – 15:00

    Play the opening

    Subscribers stream or download the first 15 minutes of the story. The session ends at the timer, with no progress carried into piracy-friendly builds.

    24 HOURS

    One day only

    The trial runs as a single-day event, like a premiere night. Everyone plays together, the internet talks about it, and the hype lands in one moment.

    AFTER THE TIMER

    Buy at a discount

    Players who want more get a time-limited launch discount to buy the full game, if Rockstar Games and Take-Two agree to offer one.

    The open letter

    This is what every signature is attached to.

    TO: Netflix Games
    CC: Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive

    We are GTA fans and Netflix subscribers. We have waited over a decade for Grand Theft Auto VI, and many of us want to feel the game before we buy it.

    We are asking for a one-day event: every Netflix subscriber gets to play the first 15 minutes of GTA VI through their existing subscription. When the timer runs out, players who are hooked can buy the full game at a launch discount.

    We know this only happens if Rockstar Games says yes. They built the game and the decision is theirs. This petition shows them how many people would show up, and how many would buy.

    Fifteen minutes. One day. Give us a taste of Leonida.

    The signers

    Questions

    Is this official?

    No. This is an independent fan campaign. It is not run by, endorsed by, or connected to Netflix, Rockstar Games, or Take-Two Interactive. A trial only happens if they decide to offer one.

    Can I actually buy GTA VI here?

    No. Nothing is sold on this page and no payment is collected. The "I'd buy at a discount" box is a pledge that shows Rockstar how many trial players would convert. If a discount is ever offered, it will come from official stores.

    Why 15 minutes and one day?

    It is long enough to feel the world and short enough to protect the story. A single day turns it into a shared event instead of a demo people forget.

    What happens with my signature?

    When the count reaches meaningful milestones, the total, the pledge numbers and a selection of comments are sent to Netflix and Rockstar Games through their public contact and social channels.

    What do you do with my email?

    It is used to remove duplicate signatures and, if you ticked the box, to tell you when there is news. It is never displayed, sold or shared.
